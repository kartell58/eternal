# Server Rules

## 1. Respect
Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, threats, and abusive behavior are not allowed.

## 2. No Discrimination
Do not discriminate against or target others based on race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, health condition, or similar personal characteristics.

## 3. Appropriate Content
Do not share illegal content, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, malware, phishing links, or spam.

## 4. Privacy and Safety
Do not share personal or confidential information about yourself or others without consent. Follow platform safety guidelines.

## 5. Moderation
Follow staff instructions and use the appropriate channels. Moderation actions may be taken for rule violations, including warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans depending on severity and context.
