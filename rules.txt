# Room222 Server Rules Policy

## 1. Mutual Respect
All members must act respectfully. Abusive behavior, insults, harassment, or any form of intimidation will not be tolerated.

## 2. Prohibition of Discrimination and Hate
Any type of discrimination, prejudice, or hate speech based on the following is strictly prohibited:
- Race, ethnicity, or color;
- Gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation;
- Religion or beliefs;
- Disabilities or health conditions.

## 3. Inappropriate Content
Sharing content that includes the following is not allowed:
- Pornography or explicit nudity;
- Graphic violence or promotion of illegal activities;
- Malicious links, phishing, or spam.

## 4. Moderation and Sanctions
- The moderation team has the authority to warn, mute, kick, or ban members who violate these rules;
- Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

## 5. Communication and Feedback
- We encourage constructive feedback and suggestions, always respecting others;
- Complaints about other members' actions or moderation should be reported privately to the support team.

> Follow Discord's Terms of Service: https://discord.com/terms

-# By participating in the Room222 server, you agree to follow these rules and acknowledge that the team reserves the right to apply sanctions as necessary to maintain a safe and respectful environment.
